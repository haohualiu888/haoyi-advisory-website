- The six locations are published as strategic China landing regions following user confirmation.

  hospital access, investment, subsidy eligibility, or preferential treatment.
- Quantitative facts retain their stated reporting period.
- Planned investment and planned floor area are described as plans, not completed capacity.
- Project-specific eligibility and terms require current confirmation.
